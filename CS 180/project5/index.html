Programming Project #5: Fun with Diffusion Models

Overview

This webpage summarizes my results for Project 5. Part A explores pretrained diffusion models (DeepFloyd IF), including sampling loops, CFG, SDEdit-style editing, inpainting, visual anagrams, and hybrid images. Part B trains UNets on MNIST for denoising and flow matching, including time-conditioning and class-conditioning.

No Scheduler (Simplified Training)

From my code: To compensate for removing the exponential LR scheduler, I changed: Used a lower constant learning rate (lr=3e-3 instead of 1e-2) so training stays stable without decay. Added light weight decay (1e-4) to reduce over-updating and help generalization. Added gradient clipping (1.0) to prevent occasional big updates from destabilizing later epochs.
